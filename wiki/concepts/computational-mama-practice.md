# Computational Mama Practice

## Summary
Computational Mama is the artistic/practice name used by [Ambika Joshi](../entities/ambika-joshi.md) for her work as a tech artist and creative technologist, exploring themes of code as care, motherhood/AI intersections, and non-Western feminist datasets.
